  • Δx=b−an.\Delta x=\frac{b-a}{n}.Δx=nb−a​. It is 1n\frac{1}{n}n1​ only when the interval has length 111.

Exam-day habits

  • Write Δx\Delta xΔx and xkx_kxk​ first, then f(xk)f(x_k)f(xk​). Most mistakes happen in these two lines.
  • Take constants out of every sum before you use a partial-sum formula, and remember ∑k=1nc=cn.\sum_{k=1}^{n}c=cn.∑k=1n​c=cn.
  • Give SnS_nSn​ as a formula in nnn, then take the limit. If you know an antiderivative, check the value with F(b)−F(a)F(b)-F(a)F(b)−F(a).
  • Keep answers exact: 163\frac{16}{3}316​, not 5.335.335.33.
